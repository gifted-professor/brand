# 品牌联名平台 VI 体系 V3.1
# Brand connection platform - Visual identity V3.1

2026-09-05 · 综合评审后的最终建议体系 / Consolidated recommended system

本版确定设计方向、图形规则和执行资产。正式平台名称待确定，Brand Relations 是工作字标；“回应 / Response”是设计概念。本版不代表商标注册结论或官网已上线。前版文件保留，以本版为执行依据。

This version establishes the recommended identity, graphic rules and execution assets. Brand Relations is a working wordmark; Response names the design idea. This is not a trademark clearance or a deployed website. Earlier files remain archived; use V3.1 for execution.

## 01 品牌核心 / Brand core

**定位：帮助品牌发现伙伴、理解彼此、建立联系的平台。**

**Positioning: A platform for brands to discover partners, understand one another and connect.**

主张：让品牌之间，有了下文。

Brand line: Good connections lead somewhere.

固定品类说明：品牌联名工具 / Brand collaboration tool。

功能说明：发现值得认识的品牌，让第一次对话有方向。

Product description: Discover brands worth knowing, with a reason to start the conversation.

品牌角色是一位认真而开放的引荐者：帮助表达自己，说明值得认识的理由，让品牌自主决定下一步。品牌承诺是让联系更有内容，不保证匹配成功或合作结果。

The platform acts as an attentive, open introducer: it helps brands express themselves, explains why a connection may be worthwhile and leaves the next step to them. It promises a more meaningful starting point, not a successful deal.

人格：清楚、有判断力、好奇、平等。用清晰信息建立稳重感，用具体回应建立亲和感，用独立图形建立记忆。

Personality: clear, discerning, curious and equal. Clear information supports credibility, specific responses support warmth, and consistent graphics support recognition.

## 02 核心识别：回应双弧 / The response mark

标志由同一个弧形模块旋转 180° 形成两笔回应。外侧是开放的水平端部，内侧转向同一中心区域。弧度、笔画、方向关系固定。两笔在单色时也必须清楚区分。

Two instances of one curved module, rotated 180 degrees, form a reciprocal gesture. Horizontal outer terminals turn toward a shared center. Curvature, weight and relative direction remain fixed. Both strokes must remain legible in one color.

标志以随附 SVG 路径为准：96 × 96 单位画板，主要笔画 14 单位，标准版中心垂直间距 4 单位。实际着色边界为 x=8..88、y=18..78。净空按着色边界外至少 14 单位计算；需要时扩充容器，不能把 SVG 自带留白误当成完整净空。

The supplied SVG paths are authoritative: a 96 × 96 unit artboard, a 14-unit main weight and a 4-unit central vertical gap. Painted bounds are x=8..88 and y=18..78. Keep at least 14 units clear beyond those bounds; the SVG's built-in padding is not the full clear space.

标准版建议用于 24px 及以上；16-23px 使用专门的小尺寸版，中心间距增至 8 单位。主标志印刷建议不小于 8mm 画板宽，仍须按工艺打样。不把复杂图文组合缩成 favicon。

Use the standard mark at 24px and above. Use the dedicated small-size master at 16-23px; its central gap is widened to 8 units. Begin print use at an 8mm artboard width and proof the actual process. Never shrink a full lockup into a favicon.

主要版本为黑色正形与白色反白。黑蓝双笔用于较大品牌表达，不能将蓝色固定解释为某个参与品牌。标志保持固定，辅助图形和动效可以展开、接近、轻触；几何间距不编码合作意向。

Primary marks are black and reversed white. The black-blue version is available for larger brand expressions; blue does not identify a specific participant. The mark stays fixed while supporting graphics may extend, approach or touch. Geometry does not encode consent.

禁止：拉伸、单独改变一笔比例、添加发光或描边、旋转固定标志、用参与方数量改变笔画数量、用匹配分控制形体、在繁杂照片上直接使用低对比标志。

Do not stretch, resize strokes independently, add glow or outlines, rotate the fixed mark, change its stroke count to represent participants, drive its geometry with match scores or place it at low contrast over busy imagery.

## 03 字标与组合 / Wordmark and lockups

工作字标使用 Manrope 600；中文品类使用 Noto Sans SC 400。随附横版组合已经转为路径，渲染不依赖安装字体；仍应保留字体授权文件。正式名称确定后另建字标版本，图形与旧文件保留。

The working wordmark uses Manrope 600 with Noto Sans SC 400 for the Chinese descriptor. Supplied horizontal lockups are outlined and render without installed fonts; retain the font licenses. Create a new wordmark version when the final name is confirmed, preserving the symbol and previous files.

导航优先使用横版标志；首次接触页面同时出现品类说明。小尺寸只用符号。传播标语属于文案层，不挤进所有 Logo 组合。

Use the horizontal lockup in navigation and show the category on first-contact surfaces. Use only the symbol at small sizes. Keep the brand line in the copy hierarchy rather than adding it to every lockup.

## 04 色彩系统 / Color system

| 颜色 / Color | HEX | 角色 / Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 黑色 / Black | #111111 | 文字、标志、主结构、深色落款 / Type, mark, structure and dark closing sections |
| 白色 / White | #FFFFFF | 页面背景与开放空间 / Main canvas and open space |
| 联系蓝 / Blue | #2457F5 | 主操作与一个视觉焦点 / Primary action and one visual focal point |

执行色值严格限定为黑 #111111、白 #FFFFFF、蓝 #2457F5。层级由字号、字重、留白和线条区分；不添加纸灰、辅助灰、深蓝悬停色或渐变。首屏约 80-90% 黑白、10-20% 蓝色作为起始构图参考，不作逐像素配额。工作界面减少大面积色块。

Use exactly three execution colors: #111111, #FFFFFF and #2457F5. Establish hierarchy through size, weight, spacing and lines. Do not add paper gray, secondary gray, alternate blue hover colors or gradients. Start campaigns around 80-90% black and white, with 10-20% blue, not a pixel quota. Reduce large fills in working screens.

### 为什么保留蓝色 / Why blue stays

蓝色不是因为它天然代表信任、科技或连接。三色的依据是职责分离：黑色承担平台身份、正文和结构；白色承担阅读与开放空间；唯一的蓝色承担行动与回应。红色容易与拒绝、风险或错误反馈争夺语义，绿色容易与确认、通过或成功争夺语义，紫色会强化 AI 与先锋创意工具的预期。这些是数字产品中的相对冲突风险，不是普遍心理定律。

Blue does not inherently mean trust, technology or connection. The three-color decision separates jobs: black carries identity, content and structure; white carries reading and open space; the single blue carries action and response. Red is more likely to compete with rejection, risk or error feedback; green with confirmation, approval or success; purple can strengthen expectations of an AI or avant-garde creative tool. These are relative product-semantic risks, not universal color psychology.

联系蓝确定为 #2457F5。它位于偏钴的高饱和蓝区，在黑白画面中能形成明显能量差，又足够深，可以同时使用蓝字白底和白字蓝底。更深、更低饱和的蓝会更安静，但也更靠近黑色并容易趋向传统企业软件；偏青的蓝更接近通信与系统界面；偏紫的蓝会加强先锋与生成式工具联想。上述气质差异仍属于需要验证的设计判断；本次定色依据是当前约束下的综合平衡，而非唯一正确答案。

Connection Blue is fixed at #2457F5. Its vivid cobalt character creates a clear energy difference within black and white, while remaining dark enough for blue-on-white and white-on-blue text. A darker, quieter blue moves closer to black and toward conventional enterprise software; a cyan blue moves toward communications and system interfaces; a violet blue strengthens avant-garde and generative-tool associations. These associations remain design hypotheses. This is the selected balance for the current brief, not a universally correct color.

三方评审中，品牌视角提出降低饱和度以增加沉着感；产品与红队认为这种变化会削弱刚建立的张力，而且没有用户证据证明更稳重的色值更适合转化。最终保留 #2457F5，把“稳重”交给排版、信息、状态和一致性，而不是继续压暗蓝色。独特性由回应双弧、语言和具体联系理由共同建立，不要求蓝色独自承担。

In review, the brand perspective proposed a quieter blue. Product and red-team reviews found that this would reduce the intended tension without user evidence that it would improve conversion. The final decision keeps #2457F5 and asks typography, information, state clarity and consistency to provide credibility. Distinctiveness comes from the response mark, language and concrete reasons to connect; the color does not carry that burden alone.

静态不透明色对计算：黑/白 18.88:1，蓝/白 5.59:1，白/蓝 5.59:1。实际状态和焦点仍需验证。链接配合下划线；状态配合文字和图标。

Calculated opaque text pairs: Black/White 18.88:1; Blue/White 5.59:1; White/Blue 5.59:1. Actual states and focus require validation. Underline text links and label states with text and icons.

蓝与黑的对比度为 3.38:1，不用于蓝字黑底或黑字蓝底的普通正文。白底蓝字、蓝底白字是标准文字组合；黑底正文使用白字。蓝色不能单独表示愿意交流、已确认或成功。

Blue against black is 3.38:1 and is not used for ordinary blue-on-black or black-on-blue text. Standard text combinations are blue on white and white on blue; body copy on black uses white. Blue alone never means willingness, confirmation or success.

主操作始终使用同一蓝色；悬停通过下划线、边框或轻微位移反馈。禁用保留黑色可读文字、轮廓与原因。错误、等待、已确认使用清楚的文字和图标，不新增语义色。

Primary actions retain the same blue on hover; use an underline, border or slight movement for feedback. Disabled controls retain readable black labels, outlines and explanations. Error, waiting and confirmed states use explicit text and icons without additional semantic colors.

印刷色按目标纸张和印厂 ICC 配置转换并打样，不提供未经验证的“通用 CMYK”。蓝色的屏幕亮度不能直接视为印刷效果。

Convert and proof print colors using the target stock and printer profile. There is no unverified universal CMYK equivalent for the screen blue.

## 05 字体与版式 / Typography and layout

英文 Manrope，简体中文 Noto Sans SC，繁体中文采用对应地区的 Noto Sans TC；正式繁体界面需另配字库。正文 400，标题与按钮 600；不使用点阵、极细科技字或夸张压缩字。

Use Manrope with Noto Sans SC, and the regional Noto Sans TC for Traditional Chinese releases. Body text uses 400; headlines and actions use 600. Avoid dot-matrix, extremely thin technical or heavily compressed lettering.

| 层级 / Role | 桌面 / Desktop | 移动 / Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 传播标题 / Display | 64-88px, 1.12-1.2 | 36-44px, 1.2 |
| 区块标题 / Section | 28-40px, 1.25 | 24-28px, 1.3 |
| 正文 / Body | 16-18px, 1.6 | 16px, 1.6 |
| 辅助信息 / Supporting | 14px, 1.5 | 14px, 1.5 |

桌面 12 栏、移动 4 栏；1440px 页面外边距建议 72px，移动端 24px。间距采用 4/8/16/24/32/48/64/96。左对齐为主要阅读基准，一个画面只有一个夸张尺度焦点。正文约 28-36 个汉字每行；字号与行宽需在实际语言下校准。

Use 12 desktop columns and 4 mobile columns. Begin with 72px page margins at 1440px and 24px on mobile. Spacing steps are 4/8/16/24/32/48/64/96. Left alignment anchors reading, with one expressive scale contrast per composition. Validate line length in each language.

按钮 8px 圆角，独立机会容器 12px；内容分组优先用空白与分隔，不给每个段落套卡片。关键触控操作以至少 44 × 44 CSS px 为执行目标，移动首屏先保证品类、主张与操作可见。

Use 8px control corners and 12px corners for standalone opportunities. Prefer whitespace and separators over nested cards. Target at least 44 × 44 CSS px for key touch actions and prioritize category, value and action on the mobile first screen.

## 06 辅助图形、图像与材质 / Graphics, imagery and material

辅助图形从同一双弧母版放大与裁切，保持笔画和端部关系。最常用三种构图：相向回应、局部放大、与文字并置。宣传可以出现一笔蓝一笔黑；文字不压在形体交汇与复杂阴影上。本版使用纯色平面图形。

Scale and crop the same paired-curve master while preserving its weight and terminals. Use three main compositions: facing strokes, a close crop, or strokes alongside typography. Campaigns may use one blue and one black stroke. Keep text away from overlaps and complex shadows. This release uses solid flat graphics.

本版执行资产全部采用黑白蓝平面表达，不添加材质阴影。立体表达暂不纳入执行资产。禁止充气玩具感、镜面金属、荧光边缘和随机生成的新轮廓。前版厚重弧块仅作为探索历史，不再作为本版几何母版。

Execution assets use flat black, white and blue without material shadows. Dimensional treatments are outside this release. Avoid inflatable toys, mirrored metal, glowing edges and randomly regenerated silhouettes. Earlier heavy sculptural arcs remain historical explorations, not V3.1 geometry masters.

人文内容优先使用具体的品牌诉求、回应、作品与创作过程。人物照片需要与内容有关、来源明确。虚构示例标明虚构；不编造客户见证或已达成合作。Avatar 出现在产品发现与品牌详情中，不担任平台官网主标志或首屏主角。

Humanity comes through brand intentions, replies, work and creative process. Photography should be relevant and properly sourced. Label fictional examples and never fabricate testimonials or completed partnerships. Avatars belong in discovery and brand profiles, not the platform mark or primary homepage hero.

## 07 语气与证据 / Voice and evidence

语气是邀请式、具体、平等、有边界的。说清楚为什么值得认识，也说明还需要确认什么。避免“命定伙伴”“完美匹配”“必然成功”等替品牌作决定的表达。

The voice is inviting, specific, equal and bounded. Explain why a connection may be useful and what still needs to be confirmed. Avoid language that decides for brands or promises a perfect match.

| 场景 / Context | 推荐文案 / Recommended copy |
| --- | --- |
| 品牌主张 / Brand line | 让品牌之间，有了下文。 / Good connections lead somewhere. |
| 主操作 / Primary action | 探索合作机会 / Explore collaboration opportunities |
| 次操作 / Secondary action | 先看发现示例 / See a discovery example |
| 个性化 / Personalization | 介绍你的品牌，让发现更有方向。 / Introduce your brand to shape your exploration. |
| 机会说明 / Opportunity | 为什么值得认识？ / Why might this connection matter? |
| 未确认 / Unconfirmed | 这是初步方向，成员意向尚未确认。 / An initial direction; participant interest is not confirmed. |
| 邀请 / Invitation | 想邀请你，一起聊聊这个方向。 / Would you like to explore this idea together? |

官网首屏之后展示一条由平台解释的机会片段，包含共同方向、各方贡献、待确认问题和下一步。角色或案例只作内容证据，不放大为平台身份。这个片段不是正式客户证言，也不以匹配分替代解释。

Immediately after the hero, show one platform-led opportunity fragment: a shared direction, each side's contribution, an open question and a next step. Participants serve as evidence, not as the platform identity. This is not a testimonial, and a score cannot replace an explanation.

示例：一方有线下空间，希望丰富工作日体验；另一方有内容活动，正在寻找落地场地。可讨论“小规模工作坊”。仍需确认目标人群、时间、投入与责任分工。标注“虚构流程示例，非已达成合作”。

Example: one party offers a physical venue and wants weekday experiences; another offers content events and needs a setting. A small workshop may be worth discussing. Audience, timing, investment and responsibilities remain open. Label it a fictional workflow example, not a completed partnership.

## 08 官网体系 / Website system

导航：如何建立联系 / 探索品牌 / 开始探索。主 CTA 统一使用“探索合作机会”，允许先了解和浏览；需要个性化时再引导介绍品牌。不要让所有新访客先填长表单。

Navigation: How it works / Explore brands / Start exploring. Use Explore collaboration opportunities as the primary CTA, allowing visitors to learn and browse first. Request a brand introduction when personalization is needed.

页面顺序固定为：①平台主张与主动作；②一个具体联系理由；③两种发现方式；④从邀请到交流的过程；⑤结束邀请。首屏后的证据优先于继续展示抽象装饰。官网不公开堆砌六因子、权重或架构细节。

Page order: 1. Brand promise and primary action; 2. One concrete reason to connect; 3. Two discovery paths; 4. Invitation-to-conversation process; 5. Closing invitation. Evidence follows the hero before further decoration. Keep internal factors, weights and architecture out of the marketing narrative.

Gravity 对外解释为“有方向地寻找”；抽卡解释为“给意外一次机会”。两种入口共用平台 VI，组合卡清楚列出成员与人数。发现、愿意交流、同意方案是不同状态。

Explain Gravity as directed exploration and card discovery as a chance encounter. Both share the same identity. Combo opportunities list actual participants and counts. Discovery, willingness to talk and proposal approval remain distinct states.

## 09 动效与应用 / Motion and applications

主动作：展现 → 回应 → 停留。首页一次约 1.2-1.6 秒，反馈约 180ms，信息展开约 240ms。可用轻触或路径延续表达联系，不要求靠“不接触”解释自主权。关键文案和按钮从第一帧可用。

Motion sequence: appear, respond, settle. Use one 1.2-1.6 second hero sequence, approximately 180ms feedback and 240ms reveals. Contact or continuity may express connection; a visual gap is not required to symbolize autonomy. Core text and controls are available from the first frame.

不使用持续漂浮、碰撞爆炸、随机粒子。减少动态效果时使用静帧，保留同等内容。移动端优先轻量平面版本。文字与控件必须是实际 DOM，不烘焙到整张图片或视频中。

Avoid endless floating, explosions and random particles. Reduced-motion mode uses an equivalent still composition. Mobile favors a lightweight flat treatment. Production text and controls must be real DOM elements.

应用：官网横版、社交方版、合作邀请、项目封面、品牌介绍、协作页面与邮件。固定标志独立于成员署名；邀请明确发起方、接收方、主题与状态；项目封面带日期、版本和实际参与方。社交模板将标志、主张和主要动作留在安全区。

Applications include the website, square social posts, invitations, project covers, profiles, collaboration screens and email. Keep platform identity separate from participant credits. Invitations identify sender, recipient, subject and status; project covers include date, version and participants. Keep essential content inside crop-safe areas.

## 10 交付与验收 / Delivery and acceptance

交付：双语 PDF 与本规范、标准/反白/双色/小尺寸 SVG、横版路径字标、两张矢量传播模板、CSS tokens、字体源文件及 OFL 授权、可复现的手册生成脚本。视觉母版统一来自路径定义，未自动改动应用或上传仓库。

Deliverables: bilingual PDF and this specification; standard, reversed, two-color and small-size SVGs; outlined horizontal lockups; two vector campaign templates; CSS tokens; font sources and OFL licenses; reproducible manual builder. All masters share the same path definitions. No app changes or repository upload are performed automatically.

已做：色对计算、矢量文件结构检查、PDF 渲染和排版检查。仍需在正式名称、真实网页、目标印刷材料与用户环境下验证。正式繁体发布需加入对应字体与本地化排版。

Checks include color-pair calculations, vector structure and PDF rendering. Validate the final name, live website, print materials and target user context separately. Traditional Chinese releases require regional fonts and typography checks.

上线前用 8-12 位品牌或市场负责人做定性检查：5 秒能否理解用途；20 秒能否说明一条联系理由并找到下一步；延迟后能否辨认平台。不是统计性转化证明，不以“高级”“好看”的单项评价代替任务理解。

Before launch, conduct qualitative checks with 8-12 brand or marketing decision-makers: purpose after five seconds; a reason to connect and next action after twenty seconds; later recognition. These are not statistical conversion claims, and aesthetic preference alone is not task comprehension.

真实联系、邀请与协作后端接通前，演示与规划功能需明确标识。标志唯一性、商标可用性、正式名称和商业发布素材仍需按实际发布情况核验。

Until messaging and collaboration services are connected, distinguish demonstrations from planned features. Verify mark distinctiveness, naming, trademark availability and production assets for the actual release.
